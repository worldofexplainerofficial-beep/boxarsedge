02 — THE METERS

READ THE
FIGHT.

Winning is not only landing punches. Manage stamina, guard, combo charge and Boxer's Edge while the clock runs.

HEALTH

Survive

Head and body share a health pool. Body hooks build liver damage. Enough body work forces knockdowns that need rapid get-up taps.

STAMINA

Pace

Every punch and held guard drains stamina. Empty tanks slow attacks and open you to counters. Idle and distance recover it.

GUARD

Shell

Blocking chips the guard meter. When it breaks you are exposed. Parry with timed Z against an enemy Edge finisher.

COMBO

Chain

Land hits inside the window to charge combo. X+S spends charge for a heavier multi-hit string. Overuse puts combo on cooldown.

BOXER'S EDGE

Finish

Fill the Edge meter with landed punches. In close range, X+S (or A+X on pad) starts a dramatic finisher sequence with slow-mo and high damage.

KNOCKDOWN

Rise

On the canvas, mash punches before the count reaches ten. Later knockdowns demand more taps. Three knockdowns can end the night.

03 — GAMEPLAN

HOW TO
WIN.

Protagonist Mode starts weak on purpose. Train, earn, upgrade, then climb. The ladder does not forgive early ego.

01

Train before you climb

Gym sessions award XP and points. Raise strength, speed, endurance, accuracy, footwork, defence and head movement until your OVR can handle the first rung.

02

Jab to measure

Use jabs to find range and build Edge charge safely. Commit power shots when the opponent is open or retreating to the ropes.

03

Attack the body

Hooks to the liver and solar plexus stack body damage. After enough clean body hooks, knockdowns become much more likely.

04

Break the guard

Chip a turtling opponent with pressure, then punish the guard break with uppercuts and crosses before they recover.

05

Spend Edge wisely

Boxer's Edge needs close range and a full meter. Save it for moments when the opponent is hurt or trapped — not on the first exchange.

06

Bank wins into upgrades

Defeating ladder fighters pays money for permanent upgrades. Return to the gym between tough matchups. Progression is saved across sessions.

PROTAGONIST EDGE

Basic finisher string: five jabs, one cross, then an overhand to close the cinematic sequence.

DIFFICULTY

Accessibility sliders can ease detection thresholds, stamina drain and body damage for limited mobility play.

REMATCH MINDSET

Lost a round? Reset posture, re-establish the jab, and force body work before hunting another Edge activation.
