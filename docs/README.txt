Patra combat additions:
- Q/E: 60% chance to trigger the authored Patra 'slip' animation instead of a normal side-step.
- Successful slips alternate left/right.
- Slip is invulnerable/dodge-active and costs stamina.
- Q + X / mouse left-click during an active slip queues a clean jab counter.
- Q + S during an active slip queues a clean hook counter.
- Slip counters build stun and receive a Rhythm Step counter boost.
- Rhythm Step gives Patra a short counter-speed/damage boost after evasion.
- Patra regenerates stamina 3% faster than the standard fighter baseline.
- Patra's southpaw cross is treated as her signature liver shot: it drains stamina and temporarily pauses stamina regeneration.
- Patra's combo punch has a brief invulnerable opening for her jab/slip entry.
- Patra's four-punch combo starts with a brief freeze/stun setup so the next overhand hit can land cleanly; the overhand receives increased damage and stun pressure.
- Patra's attacks build stun pressure faster.
